# Life-to-Day — Path to Today

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-10-13 and installed in the project.
Generation: built-in image_gen tool; master.png has a transparent background.

## Product and concept

The product connects long-term vision to today's next action. Sources: PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT sections 2–3, daily planning and life timeline documentation, and frontend/styles/tokens.css.

The previous logo used a circle and two separate curves. It was simple and supported monochrome use; the design assessment was that the life-to-action story and square-icon silhouette could be stronger.

The approved blue ribbon represents the continuous life path, the soft fold acknowledges a nonlinear journey, and the amber orb represents today. Blue follows the app's existing #2563EB primary color. The master artwork remains unchanged; production derivatives resize and package it for their intended context.

## Research

- [Apple — App icons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons): simple recognizable shapes, legibility at small sizes, and a consistent identity.
- [Linear — Brand guidelines](https://linear.app/brand): generous clear space and monochrome applications.
- [Things — Things for OS 26](https://culturedcode.com/things/blog/2025/09/things-for-os-26/): preserve identity across different icon appearances.

References informed principles, not copied graphics. This is an original generated design; a comprehensive trademark similarity search was not performed.

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original master logomark for Life-to-Day, a personal life operating system that connects long-term vision to today's single next action.
Primary request: Design one extraordinarily refined, original, memorable sculptural logo. Apple-caliber industrial design restraint, friendly confidence, calm focus, exceptionally controlled geometry and optical balance. This is a NEW identity, not a redesign of an existing supplied mark.
Core concept: "a life path arriving at today". The mark is a single broad, softly sculpted cobalt-blue ribbon forming an asymmetric open loop, with a small warm amber sphere at the open upper-right endpoint. The blue ribbon begins as a short upright on the left, flows through a generous rounded bottom bend into a sweeping right-hand arch and returns toward the upper center, leaving a deliberate spacious opening toward the upper right. Its overall silhouette subtly suggests a fused L and D monogram without literal lettering. The amber sphere is the present moment, aligned with the ribbon's open end, separated by one clearly visible gap. A cohesive compact symbol, only TWO components, strong memorable silhouette, large clean internal negative space. Let the ribbon feel like a tangible precision-crafted object with broad smooth faces and softly rounded edges rather than a thin wire. Make a subtle sculptural fold at the lower-left bend to give the silhouette a distinctive authored character.
Style/medium: very high-end soft 3D product rendering of a vector-friendly brand symbol; mostly frontal orthographic view, only slight depth. Satin ceramic or anodized finish, elegant broad highlights, restrained bevels, no busy reflections. The silhouette must remain recognizable if flattened to a single color.
Color palette: cobalt #2563EB through deep blue #163DA8 on the ribbon; restrained pale-blue reflections; warm golden amber #F0B44C on the sphere. No purple, no rainbow.
Composition: one isolated mark centered on a square canvas, occupying approximately 65 percent of the width and height, generous clear margins. True transparent background, clean alpha edge, no backdrop or ground plane. No text, no label, no wordmark, no mockup.
Lighting: soft large studio light from upper left, subtle ambient occlusion only within the object. Crisp premium craft, softly tactile depth, not toy-like inflated plastic, not chrome.
Constraints: original silhouette; do not imitate Apple, Linear, Things, Google, Meta or any other existing logo. No apple shape, checkmark, checklist, calendar, clock, infinity sign, mountain, rocket, arrowhead, stock swoosh, generic letter D ring, extra orbits, sparkles, watermark, frame, squircle, multiple options, presentation board or decorative objects. Maintain only one intentional gap and one orb. Prioritize beauty, visual simplicity, distinctive geometry and favicon legibility.
```
